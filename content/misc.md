        A few boring things about me (outside finance).

        ## Boxing
        After trying out a number of sports, I started boxing back in 2013 and haven't gotten bored of it yet. Consistent with my theoretical side, I do prefer technique training to actual sparring.

        ## Theater and performing arts
        Theater was probably my biggest passion from the moment I started in 2005. Unfortunately, I stopped when I left Italy. I still love all sorts of performances.

        ## Historical documentaries
        When I'm having a bad day or I'm sick, I usually like to tune into historical documentaries or podcasts. My personal favorites are those about 20th-century history, as well as the Roman Empire. My ultimate comfort treat is a documentary featuring Alberto Angela.

        ## South Tyrol
        I come from South Tyrol, a bilingual province in the Italian Alps where both Italian and German are spoken (some valleys are even trilingual, with Ladin as well). You might have heard of it thanks to Jannik Sinner. I am very grateful for my origins, and I find the history of my province and its multicultural character truly fascinating.
